This project presents the data and results of a meta-analysis described in:

Kang et al. (2025). Effects of oxytocin administration on nonsocial executive functions in humans: A preregistered systematic review and meta-analysis. *Molecular Psychiatry*, 1–13. [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02871-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02871-4) 

Use the tabs to browse the data, codebook, forest plot, funnel plot and moderator analyses. It can take a minute to load the first time, because R runs in your browser.
