**What is this task?** 
It measures how quickly people adapt when the rules change, which psychologists call cognitive flexibility.

On each trial you see two images, either faces or abstract shapes. One image usually leads to a reward and the other to a loss. Your job is to figure out which one is the rewarding image. Be careful, though: about 1 in 5 times the feedback is misleading, so one unexpected result doesn’t mean the rule has changed. After a while the rule does switch, and the previously rewarded image becomes the incorrect one. The best strategy is to switch only when you’re sure.

**How to play:** 
Press F to pick the left image and J for the right one. You’ll see feedback after each choice.

*Note:
This is a demo. No data is saved, and the on-screen instructions are in Norwegian.*
