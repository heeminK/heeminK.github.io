I'm a researcher at the Oslo University Hospital and based at the [Behavioral Neuroendocrinology Lab][BNL]. My previous work has focused on meta-analytic approaches to summarize evidence in oxytocin research and facilitating open science teaching in higher education. \
Now I am working on two projects. The first project aims to develop a platform for living meta-analyses of studies in rare neurodevelopmental diagnoses. The second project aims to determine the best way to present plain language summaries of meta-analysis results for users (i.e., caregivers of those with a rare neurodevelopmental diagnosis).

[BNL]: https://www.sv.uio.no/psi/english/research/groups/bnl/
